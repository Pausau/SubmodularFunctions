Maximizing Submodular Functions

Paul Sautner, seminar paper, Seminar: Optimization (Prof. Hermann Schichl), University of Vienna, April 2023

The paper treats the maximization of nonnegative monotone submodular set functions under a cardinality constraint and under a matroid constraint, in the value-oracle model, and proves the approximation guarantees of the standard algorithms: greedy (1−1/e), thresholding, greedy under a matroid (1/2), continuous greedy with pipage rounding, and an accelerated continuous greedy.
